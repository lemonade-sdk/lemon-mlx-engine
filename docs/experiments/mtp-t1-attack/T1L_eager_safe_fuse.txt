# Technical Overview of the Fourier Transform for Engineers

## 1. Core Concept
The **Fourier Transform (FT)** is a mathematical operation that decomposes a function of time (a signal) into its constituent frequencies. It reveals the frequency spectrum of a signal, showing which frequencies are present and their respective magnitudes and phases.

*   **Time Domain:** Signal $x(t)$ varies with time.
*   **Frequency Domain:** Signal $X(f)$ varies with frequency.

The transform is invertible, meaning you can reconstruct the original time-domain signal from its frequency representation.

## 2. Mathematical Definitions

### Continuous-Time Fourier Transform (CTFT)
For a continuous-time signal $x(t)$, the Fourier Transform is defined as:

$$
X(f) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x(t) e^{-j 2\pi f t} \, dt
$$

The inverse Fourier Transform (IFT) recovers the time-domain signal:

$$
x(t) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} X(f) e^{j 2\pi f t} \, df
$$
