# Technical Overview: The Fourier Transform for Engineers

## 1. Introduction

The **Fourier Transform (FT) is a fundamental mathematical tool in signal processing, communications, control systems, and physics. It decomposes a function of time (a signal) into its constituent frequencies.

For engineers, the FT is not just a theoretical concept but a practical instrument for:
- Analyzing signal spectra
- Designing filters
- Solving differential equations
- Compressing data
- Understanding system behavior in the frequency domain
