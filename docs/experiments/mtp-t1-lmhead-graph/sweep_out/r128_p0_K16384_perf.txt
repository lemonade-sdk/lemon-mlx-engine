# Technical Overview: The Fourier Transform for Engineers

## 1. Introduction

The **Fourier Transform (FT)** is a fundamental mathematical tool in engineering that decomposes a function of time (a signal) into its constituent frequencies. It reveals the frequency spectrum of a signal, transforming it from the **time domain** to the **frequency domain**.

### Why It Matters
- **Signal Analysis**: Identify dominant frequencies, noise, and harmonics.
- **System Design**: Design filters, modulators, and communication systems.
- **Data Compression**: JPEG, MP3, and video codecs rely on frequency-domain sparsity.
