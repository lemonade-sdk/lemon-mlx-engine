# Technical Overview: The Fourier Transform for Engineers

## 1. Introduction

The **Fourier Transform (FT)** is a fundamental mathematical tool in signal processing, communications, control systems, and physics. It decomposes a function of time (a signal) into its constituent frequencies.

**Core Concept:** Any continuous, non-periodic signal can be represented as a sum of infinite sinusoidal components (sine and cosine waves) with different frequencies, amplitudes, and phases.

> **Engineering Intuition:** While the Time Domain tells you *when* something happens, the Frequency Domain tells you *what* is happening.
